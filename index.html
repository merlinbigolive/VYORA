GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Are appointments available at homes? +

No. VYORA is for hotel appointments only. Home visits are not offered.

Who can create an account? +

Adults aged 18 or over. Customer and employee accounts are subject to identity and eligibility checks.

How does therapist payment work? +

After a booking is completed and verified, the therapist's net earnings become available. The therapist submits a manual payout request; an administrator reviews and marks the payment as paid after making the transfer.

Are the profiles shown here real? +

No. These are demonstration profiles for the design preview. Real profiles must be added with the person's permission and verified credentials.
